My mom thinks I am a fast thinker so I struggle to be in the moment. My mind quickly wanders to unrelated things if I am conscious and doing something. Which means I am only partially attentive on the task at hand. This is even more pronounced while doing activities I do everyday like driving, eating or showering. Those activities are mostly happening on autopilot.

The problem with being half attentive is it wastes a lot of energy. Because your brain is trying to complete the task at hand and also analysing those random thoughts. Your performance on the task also suffers. I can drive alright but my reaction time to unexpected events on the road is greatly degraded. Or if I am talking to someone, I will fail to pick up on the subtle things the person is hinting at or comprehend the gravity of their stories and such. My satisfaction from the task also degrades. I dont get the same joy or happiness I would get if I was fully engaged in the task. For example playing tennis is very joyful if you are in it 100% and feeling every ball. Very rarely such thought meandering can lead to creative ideas but its very rare and its better to dedicate some special time for it than to do it all the time.

So this thought meandering is an energy suck, greatly degrades your performance and your satisfaction. So how do we stop it. Mostly through practice. By deliberately practicing thinking about nothing. It develops the mind muscle away from the default state of thought meandering. And just reminding yourself once in a while about staying in the moment and being there 100%. Until you keep getting better at it and your default becomes being in the moment.
