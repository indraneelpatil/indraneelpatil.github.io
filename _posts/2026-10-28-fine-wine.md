Theres some people who are finished pieces, they are good at a number of things and they look almost effortless while doing life. And then there are people who are not good at those things but are quick learners, good at taking feedback, course correcting and they learn well from experience. I definitely fall into the second category. In fact I think I am pretty late at most things in life compared to my peers. I am a late bloomer. The age at which I started investing, the age at which I had my first girlfriend, the age at which I actually had saved money in my bank account, the age at which I started playing good tennis, the age at which I stood for elections was always later than other friends my age. And I learnt pretty early because of sports that if I practice something then I can get better at it. So even though I am bad at something I dont give up easily, especially if that something is actually important to my aspirations. So thats that, thats my philosophy in life.

I try to do this in a number of different ways. Most prominently I journal every weekend where I reflect on how my week went and what I could have done better. What I can aim to do in the following week. I do new years resolutions where I reflect on the goals I had set for the year before and how many I actually accomplished and how many I didnt. I also do a work focussed retrospective once a month to introspect on where my work is headed and what I can start doing to be more efficient with my time and goals. 

I also have a learning focus. I love reading books. I try to read in my reading corner every night before sleeping. The book genre can be anything but its usually something I know nothing about or just introduce my brain to new information. My to read book list is infinite at this point and I am definitely not getting through it in this lifetime but hey who knows? I also listen to podcasts when I am cooking or in the gym. The idea of this is also very similar to reading books. And then at a high level I try to learn one new thing every year. Last year it was snowboarding. This year its Japanese. Learning something new stimulates your brain like nothing else and it is also very rewarding and adds meaning to your life like you wouldnt expect. A lot of times the journals and retrospectives also informs what to learn about but often it doesnt. Learning is also fun just for the sake of it.

My aim with all this is to the be the best version of myself and of course if I do it right I will never get there but just embrace the process of trying to be the best version of myself. You will be able to say if five years ago I knew I was doing this comfortably right now I would have thought I am lying to myself. The version you become is usually beyond your wildest imagination. And through the best version of yourself you can experience life to the fullest.
